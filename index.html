What does the ratio mean?
Contrast is measured from 1:1 (no difference) to 21:1 (maximum). The higher the ratio, the easier text is to distinguish from its background.
02
What are AA and AAA?
WCAG levels describe minimum contrast. AA is the common target: 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text. AAA is stricter: 7:1 and 4.5:1.
03
What does this check?
ContrastKit follows the WCAG relative-luminance formula for opaque sRGB colors. It is a useful check, not a substitute for reviewing an entire interface.
